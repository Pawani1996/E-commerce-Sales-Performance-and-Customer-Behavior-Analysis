# Stakeholder Analysis

## 1. Project Name
E-commerce Sales Performance & Customer Behaviour Analysis

## 2. Purpose

The purpose of this stakeholder analysis is to identify individuals involved in the project, understand their responsibilities and determine their expectations.

## 3. Stakeholder Register

| Stakeholder | Role | Responsibilities | Expectations |
|---|---|---|---|
| CEO | Business Decision-maker | Approves business strategies | Increase revenue and profitability |
| Sales Manager | Sales Operations | Monitors sales performance | Identify best- and worst-performing products |
| Marketing Manager | Marketing | Develops marketing strategies | Understand customer purchasing behaviour |
| Finance Manager | Finance | Monitors financial performance | Track revenue and sales trends |
| Business Analyst | Analysis | Gathers requirements and analyses data | Deliver actionable business insights |

## 4. Stakeholder Expectations

- CEO: Wants data-driven recommendations to improve business performance.
- Sales Manager: Wants to understand product sales performance.
- Marketing Manager: Wants to identify customer purchasing patterns.
- Finance Manager: Wants accurate revenue reporting.
- Business Analyst: Wants to understand business needs and provide solutions.

## 5. Conclusion

Effective stakeholder communication is essential to ensure that the project meets business objectives and delivers valuable insights.
