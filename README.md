# E-commerce-Sales-Performance-and-Customer-Behavior-Analysis

Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing the sales performance and customer purchasing behaviour of a fictional e-commerce company, xyz Online Retail Ltd.

The objective is to identify business challenges, analyse sales trends, understand customer behavior and provide data driven recommendations to improve business performance.

Business Problem

xyz Online Retail Ltd. is experiencing inconsistent sales performance and lacks sufficient insight into customer purchasing patterns and product performance.

The company requires a structured business analysis to identify revenue trends, evaluate product performance and understand customer purchasing behaviour.

Project Objectives

1.Analyse sales and revenue trends.

2.Identify top and low performing products.

3.Understand customer purchasing behaviour.

4.Identify opportunities to improve customer retention.

5.Provide actionable business recommendations.
